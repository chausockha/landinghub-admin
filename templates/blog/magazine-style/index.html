The Terawatt Compute Decade: Mapping Global AI Infrastructure CapEx 2020–2030

Why transformer substations, high-voltage interconnects, and baseload nuclear PPAs have overtaken lithography as the binding constraint on frontier intelligence.

ER
Dr. Elena Rostova & Marcus Vance
Meridian Macro & Grid Desk · Zurich / SF

Every industrial revolution eventually collides with civil engineering. Between 2020 and 2026, capital expenditure across the six largest hyperscalers quadrupled from $84 billion to $312 billion annually—the fastest private capital mobilization since nineteenth-century trunk rail. Yet our audit of 140 regional transmission queues reveals a stark divergence between silicon delivery schedules and megawatt energization.

"A cluster of 100,000 next-generation accelerators can be assembled in nineteen weeks; the 345kV substation feeding it now waits fifty-one months."
